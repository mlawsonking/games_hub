Undertow

You have no weapon: the whole screen is a live fluid, and you dash to stir it so the water itself tears the enemies apart.

About

Every dash leaves a wake and a whirl in glowing dye. Enemies drift with the water and are only hurt by fast or shearing water, by hard crashes into walls or each other, and by the eye of a strong whirl. Still water keeps them safe, and the calmer it gets, the faster more of them arrive.

Lime drifters break in any rough water, orange heavies only in a whirl's eye or a hard crash, and magenta splitters split in two. Four hits end the run; quick kills chain for more score, and every fight leaves a different painting.

What the frame allows

  • allow-scripts The game is JavaScript (ES modules and WebGL2).
  • allow-same-origin Its module scripts load from its own origin, and its best score and mute setting live in localStorage.
  • allow="gamepad" Gamepad aiming and dashing. Cross-origin frames get no gamepad input without it.

Everything else is off: no pop-ups, no dialogs, no navigating this page. The game never sees this hub's address.
