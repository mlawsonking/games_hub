Midnight Snack

A co-op contraption builder for the family TV: chain the sleeping household's habits to get the cookie to the kids' bed without waking the grown-up.

About

Pass the remote. Each player places one piece, then someone holds go and the whole chain runs by itself. The parts are the household: the cat always chases the red dot, the dog fetches whatever lands by its bed and carries it to the kids, a string opens a door quietly, and too much noise wakes the grown-up.

Each house opens with a wordless picture of the goal and a tour of the creatures' habits, in thought bubbles. The first house is won with one piece: the red dot by the jar. Four houses, one to four players, and a failed run rewinds and points at the link that never happened.

What the frame allows

  • allow-scripts The game is JavaScript (ES modules and canvas).
  • allow-same-origin Its module scripts load from its own origin, and the solved-house stars live in localStorage.
  • allow="gamepad" Made to be played with a gamepad on the TV. Cross-origin frames get no gamepad input without it.
