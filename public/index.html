A Question of Severance

A turn-based roguelike: you are a maintenance robot waking inside a factory that sealed its doors and severed its staff nineteen years ago.

Phone: no. Needs a keyboard. It has no touch controls, and its title screen is clipped at phone width. Best on a computer.

A pair · two games, one sea

Tidepool, two ways

The same evolving sea twice. In Tidepool you watch it and nudge the water; in Red Queen you harvest it, and it adapts to how you play.

simulation · evolution · toy · 2026

Tidepool

A small ocean that runs itself: creatures with genomes graze, breed, mutate and die, and you only nudge the water.

Phone: yes. Works on phones: tap and drag both act on the water, and nothing scrolls sideways at 390 px. The trait panel is hidden on narrow screens.

arcade · evolution · touch · 2026

Red Queen

A co-evolutionary harvest game: every strike is natural selection, so the pool learns you. Herd its genome into a corner, then exploit the open flank.

Phone: yes. Plays with taps on a phone. At 390 px the tool bar loses a few pixels at each edge, and the power draft shows only its middle card in full; the other two run off the screen.
