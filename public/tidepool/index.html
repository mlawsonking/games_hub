About

A closed ecosystem. Every creature carries a genome of speed, perception, size and restlessness, and pays a metabolic price for its gifts. Nothing is scripted; whatever the pool looks like in ten minutes, it evolved that way.

Drop nutrients, stir a current, or call a storm. The pool hums if you turn the sound on, and its pitch follows the population. It is a toy more than a game: there is no score and no end.
