About

You are the Factor at the head of the pit. Parties go down with your written orders and come back with reports, and not every line in a report is true.

Mark each line trusted, doubted or rejected, and keep the stores, the map and the people straight across a six-week season.

What the frame allows

  • allow-scripts The game is JavaScript (ES modules).
  • allow-same-origin Its save and settings live in localStorage, and its module scripts must load from its own origin.
  • allow-forms The seed field and the room rename are forms whose submit handlers call preventDefault; without it Enter in the seed field would not start a season.

Everything else is off: no pop-ups, no dialogs, no navigating this page. The game never sees this hub's address.
